TwemojiMozilla.ttf
==================

A COLR/CPAL (colour-layer) build of the Twemoji emoji set, produced by Mozilla's
twemoji-colr project. Vendored here as TwemojiMozilla.ttf.

  Font build:  https://github.com/mozilla/twemoji-colr   (release v0.7.0)
  Artwork:     https://github.com/twitter/twemoji

Why this font is shipped
------------------------
The renderer draws colour glyphs from COLR/CPAL layer tables. The emoji fonts the
platforms ship are mostly NOT that format: Noto Color Emoji on Linux and Android is
CBDT/CBLC (colour bitmaps) and Apple Color Emoji on macOS is sbix, so emoji could not
be drawn there at all. Shipping one COLR face makes emoji render identically on every
platform.
